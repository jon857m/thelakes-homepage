Iconic viewpoints

Woodland & waterfalls

The 10 walks

A quick rundown — perfect for planning (or saving for later).

Catbells

A popular fell with rewarding views. The start is steep but manageable; keep dogs on a lead near the summit.

Grasmere

A gentle, scenic loop around the lake — great for a relaxed walk, with paddling spots.

Derwentwater

Another lovely lakeside walk with options to extend; some boat trips are dog-friendly too.

Aira Force

Spectacular waterfall trails through woodland — keep dogs on a lead near steep drops.

Borrowdale

Valley walking with woodland, riverside paths and open fell options to suit different abilities.

Buttermere

Relatively easy and flat around the lake, with stunning surrounding fells.

Helvellyn via Striding Edge

Challenging and only for experienced owners + confident dogs. Narrow ridge with steep drops — lead required.

Tarn Hows

Picture-perfect tarn with easy, accessible paths — brilliant for a picnic day.

Grizedale Forest

Loads of trails plus sculptures along the way — great variety and options.

Ullswater

Shoreline walking and routes into the surrounding fells around the second largest lake in the District.

Before you go

A few simple things that make the day smoother for everyone.

Leads & livestock

Keep dogs on leads near livestock, on busy trails, and around cliff edges.

Weather changes fast

Check the forecast and pack for shifts — the Lakes can turn quickly.

Leave no trace

Bring bags, dispose responsibly, and respect wildlife and habitats.

Parking can be limited in peak season — arriving early (or using public transport where possible) helps. Paths can be rocky or uneven, so good footwear is worth it.
